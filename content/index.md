I am an independent researcher and programme evaluator, and co-founder and Director of [Causal Map Ltd](https://causalmap.app) in Bath.

## What I work on

- [[causal-mapping/index|Causal mapping]]: coding what people say about what causes what, and building maps you can search and quote from. This is the main thing.
- [[ai-and-evaluation|AI for evaluation and social research]]: the [Causal Map](https://app.causalmap.app) app, and [[qualia|QualiaInterviews]], an AI interviewer.
- [[theories-of-change/index|Theories of change]], including the free [Theorymaker](https://theorymaker.netlify.app) app.
- [[methods/index|Methods and reproducible research]]: reproducible reporting in R, rubrics, survey analysis.
- [[projects|Projects and clients]]: every evaluation and research project since 2001, filterable by theme and organisation.

## Bio

I was based in Sarajevo from 1997 to 2017 and did a lot of research in Bosnia and Herzegovina and the wider Balkans. There I co-founded [proMENTE social research](http://proMENTE.org). Before that I ran a series of academic and training projects for students and psychosocial professionals in South-East Europe, for the University of Munich with [Prof. Willi Butollo](http://butollo.de).

I have worked in about 35 countries, mainly South-East Europe, South and South-East Asia and the Pacific, and sub-Saharan Africa, on topics from disaster response in Haiti and psychosocial programming after the 2004 tsunami to social capital in the former Soviet Union and counting stray dogs in Sarajevo. As a freelancer I often worked for international development and disaster recovery programmes, including several years as Lead Researcher and Statistician on the IFRC ["Everyone counts"](https://data.ifrc.org/everyone-counts-report-and-analysis) global reports.

My academic research was mainly in clinical psychology after war, covering post-traumatic stress disorder and [post-traumatic growth](https://doi.org/10.1002/jclp.10117), mostly with colleagues in Sarajevo and Munich, especially [Maria Hagl](http://lmu-munich.academia.edu/MariaHagl). My [PhD](https://eprints.mdx.ac.uk/8402/1/StephenPowellPhD.pdf) sums that up. I studied philosophy, maths and psychology.

Now I live in Clevedon, near Bristol.
