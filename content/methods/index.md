date: 2026-08-15

Almost all the quantitative work I do is reproducible: the report is generated from a source file, so the tables and charts are rebuilt from the raw data every time it is saved. Clients get consistent numbers and can check the calculations themselves.

The posts here cover that workflow, how to explain it to a client, rubrics and scales, and a few analyses I was pleased with at the time.
